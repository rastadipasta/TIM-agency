What we store

This website uses browser localStorage and sessionStorage. These are not traditional HTTP cookies, but similar forms of device storage. Our application currently uses no analytics or marketing cookies. Essential storage supports website operation; optional language storage is enabled only after your choice.

Your choice

Choose Accept All, Reject All or Customize in the banner. Rejection keeps only essential storage. Change your choice at any time using the button below or Cookie settings in the footer. Withdrawal removes the saved language; the website remains available.

External services

Fonts are served from our own domain without sending requests to Google. Vercel provides hosting. Our privacy policy explains these services and enquiry processing. If we introduce optional services, we will update this policy and request the appropriate choice before enabling them.
