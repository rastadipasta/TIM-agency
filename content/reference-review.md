# Reference alignment review

Compared all four supplied references with the corresponding English pages at
1672 × 941, then checked the same layouts in Croatian and at smaller widths.

| Area | Difference found | Correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shared header | Divider too low, logo too compact, navigation shifted right | Matched the 78 px header, relaxed logo tracking and moved the desktop navigation left. |
| Homepage headline | Excessive negative tracking made the heading narrow | Restored the reference's broader line lengths, with a bold first line and lighter following lines. |
| Homepage copy and actions | Paragraph and stage row ran too low | Matched the paragraph's three-line rhythm, 64 px CTA height and stage row near y=770. |
| Web Design | Headline too small; copy and CTA too low | Enlarged the headline, matched the larger service introduction and placed the CTA near y=585 and stages near y=745. |
| Graphic Design | Headline too narrow; body too large; stage row too low | Increased headline scale and relaxed tracking, reduced body scale and moved the stages back near y=770. |
| Digital Products | Both title lines carried the same weight | Restored the strong/light title contrast, larger product heading and reference copy width. |
| All hero artwork | Transparent asset margins made scenes too small and too far right | Positioned each 920 px canvas using its source crop coordinates, restoring the artwork's original scale and placement. |
| Background typography | Words were too dark, uniformly sized and crowded | Lightened the words, aligned their first line and gave each page its own size hierarchy. |
| Orbit and small labels | Orbit size and satellite placement differed by scene | Adjusted each scene separately; improved stage-label readability. |
| Services, work, process and statement | Excessive section spacing and compressed headings | Reduced the shared section spacing and relaxed heading tracking without rearranging sections. |
| Tablet and mobile | Artwork was undersized and microcopy was too small | Recomposed the artwork within each stacked hero, reset desktop navigation offsets and increased small labels to 10 px. |

The references show desktop heroes only. Lower sections retain their original
content and order, with spacing and typography carried through from those heroes.

Validation covers both languages, all 12 routes, desktop/mobile navigation,
accessibility and overflow, plus intermediate breakpoint checks. The page bodies
retain identical text and section order. The existing intro and black iris page
transition were not edited during this alignment pass.
