What we store

This website uses browser localStorage and sessionStorage. These are not traditional HTTP cookies, but similar forms of device storage. Google Analytics sets analytics cookies only with Analytics consent. We do not use marketing cookies. Necessary storage supports the consent and motion controls. We do not store contact-form or project-brief answers in browser storage.

Your choice

Choose Accept All, Reject All or Customize in the banner. Rejection keeps only essential storage. Change your choice at any time using the button below or Cookie settings in the footer. Withdrawal removes the saved language and analytics cookies and disables analytics; the website remains available.

External services

Fonts are served from our own domain without sending requests to Google. Vercel provides hosting. Our privacy policy explains these services and enquiry processing. If we introduce optional services, we will update this policy and request the appropriate choice before enabling them.

Contact

For questions about storage and privacy, email studio@timdsgn.com.

Google Analytics

With your consent, Google Analytics processes page visits, interactions and browser/device information for usage reports. Google provides this service. Advertising signals are disabled in our tag. Cookie lifetime is set to 180 days. You can withdraw consent in Cookie settings. See Google’s privacy policy.
